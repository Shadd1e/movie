# Human-sounding UX and writing map

The system should feel written by a considerate product team, not by a machine explaining itself.

## Voice

- Warm, direct and adult.
- Respectful toward older adults; never childish, patronising or overly cheerful.
- Prefer everyday verbs: “choose”, “try”, “save”, “change”, “search”.
- Keep explanations concrete.
- Do not repeatedly say “Based on your preferences…”.
- Do not call users “senior users” inside ordinary UI copy. Say “you” or “your choices”.
- Avoid fake certainty: say “may suit you” rather than “you will love this”.
- Avoid AI jargon in the user interface.

## Recommendation explanation patterns

Use the actual reason available from the model:

- Genre match: “It fits your interest in mystery stories.”
- Content match: “Its story and themes are close to movies you have enjoyed.”
- Collaborative signal: “Its rating pattern is close to titles that fit your taste.”
- Mixed signal: “It brings together the kind of drama you enjoy with a story similar to one of your favourites.”

Do not rotate empty synonyms just to appear human. Variation should come from the underlying reason.

## Onboarding

Bad: “Please select your preferred movie genres to optimise your recommendation profile.”

Better: “What do you enjoy? Pick the genres you would be happy to see more often.”

Bad: “Your preferences have been successfully captured.”

Better: “That gives us a good starting point.”

## Errors

Bad: “An unexpected error occurred while processing your request.”

Better: “We could not load the movies just now. Please try again.”

## Accessibility

- Large readable type.
- High contrast.
- Visible focus states.
- Large tap/click targets.
- Clear labels instead of icon-only controls for core actions.
- Avoid dense dashboards and endless horizontal carousels.
- Do not assume reduced ability simply because the user is 60+.

## Academic honesty

Never claim an evaluation result until the experiment has actually been run. Never claim that DeepSeek supplied factual movie data when the source is TMDB. Never present AI-derived classifications as objective facts.
